If you like VimStar's templates, but there are maybe a few things you want to tweak here and there, have I got a feature for you today. Customizable templates! 

Now you can define a location for your own templates that are based on VimStar's. Want to add a custom logo to the report template cover? Add a specific notice about AI on your book's copyright page? Now you can. 

[Documentation](/VimStar/writing/templates/) is, of course, available.
