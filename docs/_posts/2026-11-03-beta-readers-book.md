There's a new template for beta readers of a book in progress: the Beta Readers Book.

It formats your manuscript the same way the book template does, with a title page, copyright page, dedication, and table of contents, and chapters that start on new pages.

The difference is in the page setup. The book layout is centered on a larger page (US Letter or A4) with large margins all around so beta readers can add margin notes as they read.

Head over to the [Templates docs](/writing/templates/) for the full details. Keep on writing!
